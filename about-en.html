Our story // where we came from

AGENZA was born from the heart of the Egyptian market — from online stores losing customers because replies took 3 hours, and from comments full of buying intent that nobody answered. We tried traditional bots and watched them drive customers away with saved templates.

So we built something different: a smart agent trained on your products, your dialect and your style — one that tells an excited customer apart from a hesitant or upset one, and acts right with each.
