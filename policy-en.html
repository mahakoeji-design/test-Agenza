Privacy Policy & Terms.

Welcome to AGENZA. This page covers everything you need to know: how we protect your secrets, our payment system, monthly fees, and maintenance. Simply and plainly.

📅 Last updated: September 2026 • Applies to all AGENZA clients
// Contents 1 — Confidentiality 2 — Data we collect 3 — How data is used 4 — Payments (50% upfront) 5 — Monthly fees 6 — Maintenance & support 7 — Ownership & delivery 8 — Cancellation & refunds 9 — Client responsibilities 10 — Service limits 11 — Contact us

01 Confidentiality — your secrets are safe 🔒

This is our top priority. Our whole business runs on trust, so:

  • The company is committed to never disclosing any client secrets — no names, products, prices, data, or chats.
  • Sharing your data with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes is strictly forbidden.
  • Every team member signs a strict internal NDA.
  • Data access is limited to the smallest possible number of specialists working on your project only.
  • When the contract ends — on your request — we delete operational data copies from our systems within 14 days and send you a deletion confirmation.
  • We never use your company name or logo in our marketing without your explicit written approval.

02 Data we collect and why 📥

To build an agent that truly understands your business, we need:

  • Contact details: name, WhatsApp number, email address.
  • Business data: products, prices, offers, FAQs, and your preferred reply style.
  • Connection permissions: social pages, WhatsApp Business, email, and Google Sheets files — limited to running the agent only.
  • Chat and order logs — to improve the agent and measure results.
⚠️ We never ask for your banking passwords or your customers' payment details. The agent only handles order confirmation.

03 How we use data — and what we never do 🛡️

  • Training your agent only on your products and style — never on anyone else's.
  • Running replies, confirming orders, and logging to sheets.
  • Performance reports and weekly reply tuning.
  • We don't sell data. We don't share it. We don't use it to train public models available to any other client.
  • Connections are encrypted, and dashboards use separate passwords and per-project permissions.

  • Delaying the second payment pauses final delivery and tuning services until paid.

05 Monthly fees — paid in full at month start 🔁

After delivery, the agent needs monthly running costs to stay at full performance. The rule is simple:

  • Monthly fees are paid in full at the start of each month (days 1–5 at most).
  • They cover: AI API costs (reply consumption) + maintenance, hosting and monitoring.
  • Your plan fee is fixed; if your usage exceeds normal limits we notify you first and suggest an upgrade — no surprise bills.
  • Missing the monthly payment may lead to a temporary pause after a 48-hour notice, and service resumes immediately on payment with no restart fee the first time.
  • Extra services (new channels / languages / CRM links) are billed separately with your prior approval.
💡 Why upfront? Because we pre-pay API and hosting costs to providers on your behalf — early payment keeps your agent from stopping for a single second.

06 Maintenance — with you all month 🛠️

Clients get maintenance all month long as part of the monthly fee, including:

  • Round-the-clock agent monitoring with instant intervention on any failure.
  • Weekly reply improvements based on real chats.
  • Updating prices, products and offers when you send them (within 48 business hours).
  • Free fixes for any connection issues (WhatsApp / social / sheets).
  • A short performance report each month-end: chat counts, confirmation rate, key notes.
  • Priority WhatsApp support during working hours + emergency line for critical outages.

Maintenance does not include: building a brand-new agent, or radical changes to the business nature — billed as extra development under a separate agreement.

07 Ownership & delivery 📦

  • After full setup payment, your agent's setup (replies, knowledge base, connection files) is entirely yours.
  • AGENZA's core technical infrastructure (code and platform) remains ours, granted to you as a usage right for the subscription duration.
  • Delivery happens through a pilot run you approve yourself before official launch.
  • The agreed timeline starts from receiving the upfront payment + all required data from you.

08 Cancellation & refunds ↩️

  • The 50% upfront is non-refundable once work has actually started, as it covers build, training and connection hours.
  • If you cancel before work starts, the upfront is fully refunded within 7 business days.
  • Monthly plans can be cancelled before the new month — amounts paid for an ongoing month are non-refundable while service and maintenance continue.
  • In case of a major shortfall on our side not fixed within 14 days of reporting, you're entitled to a fair refund by mutual agreement.

09 Client responsibilities 🤝

  • Providing accurate, up-to-date data (prices, products, shipping and return policies).
  • Granting the needed connection permissions and cooperating in testing and approval.
  • Replying to our team's questions during the build phase so we meet the deadline.
  • Not using the agent for anything illegal or against platform policies (spam, fraud, banned content).

10 Service limits — honestly stated ⚖️

  • We target 99% monthly stability, but outages beyond our control can happen (WhatsApp / Facebook / API provider outages) — we act immediately and notify you.
  • AI can rarely err; that's why we set review rules and human escalation for sensitive cases (large amounts / complaints).
  • We're not liable for losses caused by wrong data you provided, or your delay in approving updates.
  • This policy may be updated with a 7-day prior notice; the version published here is the reference.
