Last updated: September 2026

--- About ---
- AGENZA is an Egyptian software company specialized in AI agents.
- Born from a real problem: stores losing customers to 3-hour reply delays and unanswered buying-intent comments, while traditional bots drove customers away with saved templates.
- Mission: every Arab business gets a 24/7 sales + support team for the cost of one employee.

--- The three agents ---
1) Smart Reply Agent: answers WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram (DMs/comments/stories), TikTok, and Facebook comments in the team's own style and dialect. Understands images, prices, complex questions. Moves comments to DM automatically. Escalates to humans when needed.
2) Order Confirmation Agent: messages every customer on WhatsApp/email after ordering; verifies name, address, phone, quantity, payment (cash/card); follows up pending orders and abandoned carts; sends a daily report: confirmed / cancelled / no-answer.
3) Data Entry Agent: every chat/order becomes an organized row in Google Sheets or Excel instantly (name/phone/address/product); cleans numbers and addresses; alerts on missing data; links with CRM and existing sheets.

--- Process (4 steps) ---
1) 30-minute call to learn the business, products, prices, style.
2) Build and train the agent; client reviews replies pre-launch.
3) Connect all channels in one day.
4) 24/7 operation with daily reports and weekly tuning.

--- Plans ---
- Launch: 1 agent (reply or confirm) + 2 channels + up to 1,000 chats/month + Google Sheet logging.
- Growth (most popular): 3 agents + all channels/comments + up to 10,000 chats/month + reports + weekly tuning + priority WhatsApp support.
- Business: custom agents + CRM/API links + unlimited chats + account manager.
